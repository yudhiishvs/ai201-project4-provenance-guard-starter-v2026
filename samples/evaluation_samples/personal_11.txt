When I work on a technical project, documentation is usually the part I want to postpone. The code feels urgent because it is what makes the program run. But after returning to an old project, I realized how quickly small decisions become difficult to reconstruct. I could remember what a function did, yet I could not remember why I had chosen a particular threshold. Since then, I have tried to record assumptions and tradeoffs while they are still fresh. I do not write a long explanation for every line. Instead, I focus on decisions that another person might reasonably question. Good documentation should help someone understand the design without requiring them to guess what I was thinking several weeks earlier.
