Yesterday I took a walk around my neighborhood after lunch. The weather was cool, and there was a little wind. I wore a jacket and comfortable shoes. I passed a park where a few children were playing basketball. A dog barked from behind a fence, but it stopped when I walked past. I did not listen to music because I wanted some quiet time. After about thirty minutes, I turned around and headed home. I felt more awake when I got back. I had been sitting at my computer for most of the morning, so the walk was a good break. I want to make this a regular habit, even on days when I have a lot of work. A short walk is easy to fit into my schedule.
