There is a particular kind of satisfaction in finishing a book that challenged me. Some books are easy to read because the plot moves quickly or the writing feels familiar. Others demand that I stop, reread paragraphs, and reconsider what I thought the author meant. I used to see that slower pace as a sign that I was not reading efficiently. Now I think the difficulty can be part of the value. When an argument is unfamiliar, I often write a short summary in my own words after each chapter. If I cannot explain the main claim, I know I need to return to the text. I do not do this with everything I read, but it helps when I want more than a vague memory of the material.
