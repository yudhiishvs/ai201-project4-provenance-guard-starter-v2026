My desk gets messy during the week. I leave notebooks, pens, receipts, and charging cables on it. By Friday, there is barely enough room for my laptop. This morning I decided to clean it. I threw away old papers and put the pens in a cup. I moved my textbooks to the shelf and wiped the desk with a cloth. I also found a library book that I thought I had lost. The whole job took about twenty minutes. Now I have more space to work, and I can find things faster. I know the desk will probably get messy again, but I want to keep it clean for at least a few days. I think putting things away each night would help.
