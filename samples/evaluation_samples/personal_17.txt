One reason I like cooking with other people is that it creates a natural division of attention. Someone can prepare vegetables while another person watches the stove, and conversation fills the gaps between tasks. The process becomes more enjoyable than cooking alone, even when the meal itself is ordinary. I remember making dinner with friends when we realized halfway through that we did not have the spice listed in the recipe. We debated substitutions, tasted the sauce, and eventually decided to leave it out. The dish turned out fine. What I remember most is not the flavor but the way everyone contributed small ideas. It was a reminder that shared activities do not need to be elaborate to become memorable.
