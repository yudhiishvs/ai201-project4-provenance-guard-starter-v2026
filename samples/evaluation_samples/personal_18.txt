When comparing two job opportunities, I think it is easy to focus too heavily on the most visible number: compensation. Salary matters, especially when living costs are high, but it does not describe the full experience. The quality of mentorship, the kind of work assigned, and the amount of responsibility can affect what someone learns. I would also consider whether a team has a clear way to review code and discuss mistakes. An impressive title might offer little growth if the role is mostly repetitive. On the other hand, a demanding environment is not automatically a good learning opportunity. I would want to understand what support exists when a new employee encounters something unfamiliar. The best choice depends on both immediate needs and longer-term goals.
