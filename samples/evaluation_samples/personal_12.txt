I enjoy visiting unfamiliar places, but I rarely plan every hour of a trip. I prefer to identify a few things I definitely want to see and leave space for unexpected discoveries. On one afternoon, I wandered away from a busy shopping street and found a small park with benches facing a river. There was nothing particularly famous about it. I stayed there longer than I had planned because it was peaceful and gave me a chance to observe the city at a slower pace. That experience changed how I think about travel itineraries. A schedule can make a trip efficient, but too much structure can turn exploration into a checklist. I still make reservations when necessary; I just try not to schedule away every possibility.
